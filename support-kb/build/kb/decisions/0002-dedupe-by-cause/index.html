Context

The reassessment of #4471 separates a shared parser defect from a booking-v3 hint defect. Subject equality and browser intake tags do not establish a common cause. Decision 0001 is retained unchanged as an accepted historical record.

Proposed replacement for decision 0001

Deduplicate report IDs first. Read the body and resolve tenant locale, form version and channel. Merge into one engineering ticket only when evidence supports the same cause. Keep unknown or contradictory reports for investigation; do not infer a cause from the subject.

Consequences

Triage needs more reading and tenant lookup, but each engineering ticket has a defensible scope and count. Re-run computed summaries on fresh exports while the desk keeps working. The support owner must approve this policy before it becomes accepted.
